# Market Intelligence Decision Sheet

## 1. Business Question

Can a smaller indie game avoid direct content-volume competition with AAA titles by targeting exploration-oriented customers with a lower-entry-price product that offers a sharply differentiated experience?

The analysis does not assume that AAA and indie games use separate wallet budgets. It tests observable customer and product signals that are consistent with that commercialization hypothesis.

## 2. Hypothesis Verdicts

| Hypothesis | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H1. Exploration-oriented segment exists | **Supported within observed sample** | Non-zero library median: Core 351.0, Contrast 155.0, AAA benchmark 100.5. Median review activity: Core 12.0, Contrast 10.0, AAA 4.0. |
| H2. AAA and indie purchases are complementary | **Unresolved** | Review data does not provide reliable ownership overlap or wallet-spend allocation. Share of Wallet cannot be directly proven. |
| H3. $5–15 supports a low-friction complementary position | **Partial / provisional** | 8 observed games fall in the $5–15 band and include highly rated commercial examples, but current price does not establish purchase-time price or causality. |
| H4. Short experiences can still achieve high satisfaction | **Supported as feasibility, not causality** | 6 games in the sample had median review-time playtime ≤8h while maintaining ≥92% sample recommendation. |
| H5. Customer needs can be discovered without pre-forced labels | **Supported** | Narrative & Emotion accounted for 16/20 (80.0%) labels in the 11-game baseline, falling to 15/26 (57.7%) after gameplay-led contrast games were added. New Gameplay Depth, Replayability, Content Longevity, and Technical Accessibility themes emerged. |

## 3. Validated Customer Value Structure

### Selection Drivers

- **Distinctive Presentation & Atmosphere** — median within-game recommendation delta +5.11%p; 12/12 games positive.
- **Emotional Narrative & Payoff** — median within-game recommendation delta +4.20%p; 11/15 games positive.
- **Replayability & Returnability** — median within-game recommendation delta +5.31%p; 5/5 games positive.

### Product Guardrails

- **Gameplay Depth & Agency** — median within-game recommendation delta -13.69%p; 11/15 games negative.
- **Technical Accessibility** — median within-game recommendation delta -20.19%p; 4/6 games negative. This signal should be interpreted cautiously because the number of compared games is smaller.

### Post-Purchase Expansion Signal

- **Content Longevity & Expansion** — median within-game recommendation delta +6.83%p; 4/4 games positive. Reviews explicitly request DLC, updates, and additional content, but this is not direct evidence of realized lifetime value.

## 4. Product Planning Interpretation

**Narrative-led products** can compete through emotional payoff, memorable characters, music, and a distinctive audiovisual identity rather than through content volume alone.

**Gameplay-led products** can compete through immediately legible core loops, replayability, meaningful variation, mastery, and continued content demand.

Across both types, a differentiated hook is insufficient when gameplay becomes shallow, repetitive, passive, or technically difficult to access.

## 5. Commercialization Decision

> Do not position the product as a smaller substitute for AAA. Target exploration-oriented customers with a low-entry-price, sharply differentiated experience that creates a clear additional purchase occasion.

The most defensible product proposition is therefore not "more content for less money," but **a compact experience with an immediately recognizable reason to choose it**.

Candidate reasons-to-choose identified in this analysis are **emotional payoff, distinctive audiovisual identity, and replayable gameplay depth**.

## 6. Evidence Boundary

- Steam reviews represent an observed reviewer sample, not all buyers.
- `num_games_owned == 0` is ambiguous and was excluded from library-breadth comparisons.
- SteamSpy owner counts are third-party estimates, not actual sales.
- Current Steam prices are not guaranteed to equal historical purchase prices.
- Recommendation and playtime associations do not establish causality.
- Actual AAA-to-indie cross-ownership, wallet allocation, and Share of Wallet were not directly observed.

Accordingly, the final Share-of-Wallet proposition remains a **commercialization hypothesis supported by behavioral proxies, not a proven causal fact**.
